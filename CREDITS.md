# Credits and artwork attribution

## Shedinja battle sprites

The included Shedinja front and back battle sprites are adaptations of **“G1SP 0292 – Shedinja”** by **BouncingPiplup**.

| Attribution field | Details |
|---|---|
| Original work | *G1SP 0292 – Shedinja* |
| Artist | BouncingPiplup |
| Original work | <https://www.deviantart.com/bouncingpiplup/art/G1SP-0292-Shedinja-1265806988> |
| Original artwork license | [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) |
| Adaptation in this mod | Background removal/transparency preparation and resizing/cropping for Gen1Recomp battle-sprite targets: 56×56 front and 48×48 back |

BouncingPiplup notes that the original artwork was influenced by SharkGuy’s version, with personal adjustments. This attribution does not imply that BouncingPiplup or SharkGuy endorses this mod.

The adapted files in `assets/sprites/` are distributed under the **same CC BY-SA 3.0 license**. When redistributing or adapting those sprite files, retain attribution to BouncingPiplup, provide the license link, and identify further changes.

The Lua code and original mod configuration are separate from the artist’s sprite license unless a file states otherwise.

## Mod implementation

The Gen1Recomp mod implementation was developed for the **inmento** project, with AI assistance. It is intentionally a standalone species-expansion experiment and should not be combined with other Pokédex or roster expansion mods.
